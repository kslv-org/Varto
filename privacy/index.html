Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VARTO DIGITAL LIMITED (“VARTO”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you use the VARTO mobile application, website and related services.

VARTO is a digital wishlist and gifting platform. Users can create wishlists, save product links, upload content, add gift preferences and share wishlists with others. VARTO does not sell products directly at first launch. Purchases are made through third-party retailer, marketplace, brand or affiliate links.

1. Who controls your personal data

VARTO DIGITAL LIMITED is the controller of personal data processed through VARTO, unless this Privacy Policy explains otherwise.

Contact details:

  • VARTO DIGITAL LIMITED, The Black Church, St. Mary’s Place, Dublin 7, D07 P4AX, Ireland
  • Email: support@varto.digital

If we appoint a dedicated privacy contact or Data Protection Officer in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy.

2. Who this Privacy Policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • people who create a VARTO account;
  • people who create, edit or share wishlists;
  • people who view wishlists shared with them;
  • people who click product, retailer, brand or affiliate links through VARTO;
  • people who contact us for support or business enquiries; and
  • visitors to our website or other online pages.

3. Age requirement

VARTO accounts are intended for users aged 16 and above. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly allow children under 16 to create accounts or submit personal data without any consent required by applicable law.

If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided personal data without the required consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete or restrict that information.

5. Personal data you choose to share

VARTO is built around wishlists. This means you may choose to add information that feels personal, such as sizes, birthday, gift preferences, photos, address details, notes or private comments.

You should only add information that you are comfortable sharing in the relevant wishlist context. If you make a wishlist public or share it with other people, those people may be able to see the content you included.

We recommend avoiding highly sensitive information in wishlist notes or public wishlist pages.

6. Special category data

VARTO is not designed to collect special category data, such as health information, religion, political opinions, biometric data, sexual orientation or similar sensitive information. Please do not upload this type of information unless it is strictly necessary and you understand that it may be visible in the context in which you share it.

If we identify special category data that is not needed for VARTO, we may delete or restrict it.

7. How we collect personal data

We collect personal data in the following ways:

  • directly from you when you create an account, create a wishlist, upload content, add product links, contact support or sign up for communications;
  • automatically when you use the app or website, including through cookies, pixels, software development kits, log files and similar technologies;
  • from third-party retailers, affiliate networks and brand partners when you click links or complete qualifying actions;
  • from app stores and platform providers where they provide app installation, crash, purchase, subscription, review or analytics information; and
  • from other users where they invite you to view a wishlist, reserve an item or interact with a shared wishlist.

Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how VARTO DIGITAL LIMITED uses cookies and similar technologies on the VARTO website, mobile application and related services.

For simplicity, this Cookie Policy uses the word “cookies” to refer to browser cookies, pixels, local storage, mobile identifiers, software development kits, tracking links and similar technologies that store information on, or access information from, your device.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may be used in mobile apps to recognise your device, remember settings, analyse usage, support security, enable affiliate attribution or measure performance.

2. How VARTO uses cookies and similar technologies

VARTO may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • keep you signed in and operate account features;
  • remember your privacy choices and app preferences;
  • secure the platform and prevent misuse;
  • understand how the website and app are used;
  • measure app performance, crashes and bugs;
  • support wishlist sharing and product link functionality;
  • track affiliate clicks and commission eligibility;
  • measure marketing campaigns where permitted; and
  • improve the VARTO user experience.

3. Types of cookies we may use

Type Purpose Consent approach
Strictly necessary cookies Required to operate the website, app, login, security, consent preferences, fraud prevention, load balancing and core wishlist features. These may be used without consent where legally permitted because the service cannot function properly without them.
Functional cookies Remember preferences such as language, region, display choices, wishlist settings or recently used features. Used with consent where required.
Analytics and performance cookies Help us understand app usage, website traffic, feature performance, bugs, crashes and aggregated user journeys. Used with consent where required.
Affiliate and attribution cookies Help track outbound product clicks, retailer redirects, campaign IDs, commission eligibility and affiliate performance. Used with consent where required. Third-party retailers and affiliate networks may also set their own cookies once you leave VARTO.
Marketing and advertising cookies May be used in the future to measure campaigns, understand referral sources, limit repetition, or support advertising and remarketing. Used only with consent where required.

4. First-party and third-party cookies

First-party cookies are set by VARTO. Third-party cookies or similar technologies may be set by service providers, affiliate networks, analytics providers, app platforms, retailers, brands or other partners.

When you click a product link and leave VARTO, the third-party website may use its own cookies and similar technologies under its own cookie policy and privacy policy.
